# Sponsor shortlist + cold outreach — Verdocast Championship

The first five partner categories to pitch [`sponsor-deck-championship.md`](sponsor-deck-championship.md) to, ranked by ease-of-yes, plus a cold email that carries the deck. Lead with the scalable voucher categories - they say yes fastest because the reward is their existing product, and the brand-safe / not-gambling wedge (vs real-money apps like ADI Predictstreet) is exactly why a sportsbook-shy brand can headline. Named brands are examples to slot the real contact into.

## The five, in order of ease-of-yes

### 1. Food delivery — the easiest yes
- **Examples:** Deliveroo, Just Eat, Uber Eats.
- **Why they say yes:** match night is a food-ordering occasion; the reward is a voucher off their own product (near-zero marginal cost); mass, brand-safe, football-timed reach.
- **Offer:** Reward partner → Round sponsor. Reward = "£X off your match-night order, powered by [brand]" for everyone who plays; a bigger code for qualifiers.
- **Tier:** Reward partner (entry) scaling to Round sponsor.

### 2. Sports retail — football-native
- **Examples:** JD Sports, Pro:Direct Soccer, Castore.
- **Why they say yes:** our audience is theirs; they want engaged young football fans without a gambling association; merch + voucher rewards are on-brand product.
- **Offer:** Round sponsor. Reward = kit/store voucher for qualifiers, signed merch or a boot bundle for the champion.
- **Tier:** Round sponsor → Title.

### 3. Coffee / office drinks — owns our B2B lane
- **Examples:** Grind, Pret (office subscriptions), or a hydration/soft-drinks brand (keep it non-alcohol to stay inclusive).
- **Why they say yes:** this is the workplace angle a real-money app legally cannot touch; sponsor the morning recap moment and the office-league format; a culture-win association for a brand that sells to offices.
- **Offer:** Reward partner. Reward = an office coffee/drinks drop for the winning workplace league; vouchers for individuals.
- **Tier:** Reward partner, B2B-flavoured.

### 4. Football media / streaming — barter over cash
- **Examples:** a football newsletter network, a football-content app, or a streaming service.
- **Why they say yes:** direct audience overlap; the "reward" is cheap digital inventory (subscription months); a natural cross-promo — they push our leagues, we hand their audience a perk. Often a no-cash media swap.
- **Offer:** Round sponsor via content swap. Reward = free subscription months for qualifiers.
- **Tier:** Round sponsor (barter).

### 5. Fan experience — the headline prize
- **Examples:** a match-ticket / hospitality provider or a football-travel brand.
- **Why they say yes:** they trade in experiences and want aspirational reach; the Championship gives them a single, ownable headline-prize story ("the [brand] final experience").
- **Offer:** Title / Reward partner. Reward = the champion's experience (tickets, hospitality, a trip).
- **Tier:** Title sponsor or headline Reward partner.

> **Hold the line (from the strategy doc):** no gambling / crypto-adjacent brands, no cash-for-play, no reward gated behind the paid unlock, exposure not member data. One wrong logo undoes the moat.

## Cold outreach email (carries the deck)

Keep it four lines. Personalise the bracketed bits per brand - swap in the tailored hook below.

**Subject:** A brand-safe World Cup moment - 15 minutes?

> Hi [Name],
>
> [Brand] could power the reward for the Verdocast Championship - the free, skill-based World Cup 2026 prediction game (no money, not gambling) where offices and fans predict every knockout match on a live bracket. It's the football moment a brand can own without going near a sportsbook: your logo on the live bracket and leaderboard, a co-branded league for your audience, and a "[Brand]-powered" prize.
>
> One-pager attached - worth 15 minutes this week?
>
> [Your name] · [calendar link] · verdocast.com

### Tailored one-line hooks (drop into the email's first line)
- **Food delivery:** "Match night is order night - [Brand] could power the reward every player unlocks."
- **Sports retail:** "Your customers are already playing - [Brand] could kit out the champion."
- **Coffee / office drinks:** "Offices are running prediction leagues right now - [Brand] could own the workplace moment."
- **Football media:** "Same audience, no overlap in offer - a content swap that hands your readers a perk."
- **Fan experience:** "One ownable headline: the [Brand] final experience for the tournament's best forecaster."

## How to run it
1. Pull the real decision-maker (brand/partnerships/marketing lead) per target - LinkedIn or the brand's partnerships inbox.
2. Send the tailored email with the one-pager attached; personalise the first line.
3. Log sends in a simple tracker (brand · contact · tier pitched · sent · reply).
4. On a reply, book the 15-min call, walk the live bracket, agree tier + prize, lock branding before the finale.

**Realistic goal:** land ONE headline or Round sponsor + ONE voucher/reward partner before the latter knockout rounds. That's enough to prove the model and seed the year-round platform.
