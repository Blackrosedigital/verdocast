# TikTok "bracket reaction" — reusable post-round format

Run once after **each round resolves** (R32 → R16 → QF → SF → Final). Same skeleton every time, swap the blanks. Faceless, screen-rec of `verdocast.com/world-cup-2026/knockouts` + VO. Pairs with [`tiktok-r32-launch-plan.md`](tiktok-r32-launch-plan.md) and [`tiktok-r32-vo-scripts.md`](tiktok-r32-vo-scripts.md).

**Angle:** *"{teams left} teams left - here's my bracket vs reality - how's yours holding up?"* Every resolved round becomes a reason to go back and check your standing.

## Structure (~18-22s)
**Hook (0-2s):** "{teams left} teams left. Here's my bracket against reality." *(e.g. "8 teams left…")*
1. **0-2s Hook** — teams-left number big; open on the round that just resolved.
2. **2-7s The carnage** — scroll the resolved round; call 1-2 results that went to form + 1 that didn't.
3. **7-12s My bracket vs reality** — where your call survived/busted. Honest scorecard: "{my hits}/{round size} still standing."
4. **12-16s Stakes ahead** — scroll to the next (empty) round; name your most/least confident tie.
5. **16-22s CTA** — "How's yours holding up? Check your standing." End card + sticker.

## Screen-record (every time)
- The **round that just resolved** (live scores + advancing teams).
- Your **busted vs surviving picks** (your prediction grid / leaderboard row), then back to the public bracket.
- The **next empty round** (anticipation). Hold the **full bracket** under the end card.

## VOICEOVER template (ElevenLabs, fill blanks)
> {teams left} teams left. Let's see how my bracket's holding up against reality. {result 1} - called it. {result 2} - called it. {upset result} - did not see that coming, and there goes one of my picks. So that's {my hits} of {round size} still alive after the {round}. Not perfect, but I'm still in it. Next up: the {next round}, and {one bold call for next round}. Here's the real question - how's your bracket doing? The whole thing's live at verdocast dot com. Go check where you stand before the next one kicks off.

## Caption template
> {teams left} teams left after the {round}. My bracket vs reality: {my hits}/{round size} survived - {one-line gut punch}. How's yours holding up? Full live bracket below 👇

**Hashtags (rotate team tags):** #WorldCup2026 #{round} #bracket #footballtok #predictions
**Sticker (#8):** `How's your bracket? Check your standing ↓` → CTA `verdocast.com/play?ref=tiktok` (drives to their league row); bracket page secondary.
**Sound:** "stating facts" beat for R32/R16; shift to higher-tension for QF/SF/Final (audio escalates with stakes).

## Cadence
- **One Reaction per resolved round**, the **morning after the round's final tie finishes** (the per-match Reaction slots already cover daily drama; this is the weekly "zoom out, check your standing" beat).
- R32 → after the last R32 tie (early July); then R16, QF, SF as the bracket narrows; **Final** = a wrap-up variant ("my bracket vs the whole tournament").
- Keep the 2-3 posts/day cap; on a round-resolution morning, the Reaction is the priority post.

## Production tip
Lock this as a **saved CapCut project** so each round is a 10-minute swap-the-blanks job, not a rebuild. One clean ~24s bracket scroll capture feeds both the "bracket is live" launch video and the first Reaction.
