# The Verdocast Championship - Sponsor Partnership

**The free, global World Cup 2026 prediction competition. Skill-based. No money in play. Not gambling.**
*A brand-safe context to reach engaged football fans at the peak of the tournament - a place a sportsbook-shy brand can actually be.*

---

### The one-line pitch

The Verdocast Championship is a free, skill-based prediction competition running through the World Cup 2026 knockouts. Players predict scores, climb a live leaderboard, and the best forecasters win real rewards. There is no entry fee and no pooled money - the winner is decided by skill, and the reward is funded by a sponsor. **You fund a prize; you own the moment.**

### Why now - and why this is safe for your brand

- **Peak attention.** The knockouts (from 28 June) are the most-watched, most-talked-about stretch of the tournament. Players return daily to check the board, lock picks, and chase a "Q" spot in the finale.
- **The brand-safe wedge is the whole point.** Real-money prediction apps (ADI Predictstreet and the like) run on stakes and payouts. Verdocast runs on skill, for free. That is not a caveat - it is the reason a brand that will not touch a sportsbook *can* headline here. **No gambling language, no money in play, inclusive and editorial in tone** - The Athletic, not DraftKings.
- **One negotiation, one clean story.** Instead of scattered ad buys, you own the finale of the competition and the winner story that comes out of it.

### Who plays

- **Offices and teams (B2B).** HR and internal-comms leaders run leagues for their people - a low-effort culture win for hybrid teams. Reached cleanly through the person who *wants* to share it.
- **Consumer and creator fans.** Individuals and football creators running leagues for friends and communities.
- **The profile:** engaged, opted-in, returning daily through the knockouts. **Inclusive and non-laddish by design** - the game rewards score prediction, not football trivia, so non-fans play and win too.
