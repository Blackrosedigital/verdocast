# Competitive Analysis: Verdocast vs ADI Predictstreet

**Prepared by:** Ramon (Marketing) · **Date:** 6 Jul 2026 · **Confidence:** High on identification

---

## TL;DR

The founder asked about "ADI Predictions Street." The entity is almost
certainly **ADI Predictstreet** - FIFA's **Official Prediction Market Partner**
for the 2026 World Cup. It is a **real-money prediction market** (binary
event contracts settled in a US-dollar stablecoin on its own blockchain), not
a free office game and not a content channel.

**Bottom line:** it is *not a direct competitor*. It plays in a different
category (regulated real-money trading) for a different buyer (an individual
staking money on outcomes). It is a **threat to attention and to the word
"prediction," not to our product**. The right move is to lean *harder* into
the exact things it can't be: free, inclusive, group-run, workplace-safe, and
explicitly not gambling. Our "no money, real fun" position gets *stronger* the
bigger ADI's regulated-trading brand gets.

---

## Step 1 - What is ADI Predictstreet?

A **consumer prediction-market platform**. Users trade binary "yes/no"
contracts on match and tournament outcomes (match results, tournament futures,
player-performance derivatives). Contracts price between $0.01-$0.99 and settle
at $1.00 (true) or $0.00 (false) - i.e. you put money in and win or lose it
based on the outcome. Settlement is in **USD1, a dollar-pegged stablecoin**, on
its own **ADI Chain** blockchain.
([sportshandle](https://sportshandle.com/adi-predictstreet/),
[igamingbusiness](https://igamingbusiness.com/prediction-markets/adi-predictstreet-launches-prediction-market-ahead-world-cup-2026/))

| Dimension | What we found |
|---|---|
| **Format** | Real-money trading app (mobile + desktop), fiat or crypto funding |
| **What it does** | Trade binary event contracts on WC2026 matches/players |
| **Who it's for** | Individual football fans who want to stake money on live outcomes |
| **Monetization** | Market-maker/exchange economics on real-money trades |
| **Backing** | Built by Abu Dhabi-based **ADI Chain**; CEO **Dimitrios Psarrakis** |
| **Credibility** | **Official FIFA WC2026 Prediction Market Partner** (bought a premier sponsorship package - stadium boards on broadcast) |
| **Regulatory** | Licensed in **Gibraltar (26 Mar 2026) as a betting intermediary**; went live **8 Jun 2026** |
| **Geography** | UK, Brazil, Europe direct; **US blocked** (CFTC) - Americans reach it only indirectly via **Fanatics Markets** / Crypto.com clearing in 23 states |
| **Partnerships** | **Kalshi** strategic partnership (late Jun 2026); **Chainlink** oracle; **Fanatics Markets** US distribution |
| **Reach** | **No public user numbers disclosed** - do not invent any |

**Honest caveats:** no follower/user counts are published, so I have not
estimated any. It is described in press coverage as "little-known" outside the
FIFA-partner headline. It positions itself as "informed predictions, not
chance-based betting" - but it is licensed as a betting intermediary and settles
real money, so functionally it is **gambling-adjacent**. That framing gap is the
single most important fact for us.

## Step 2 - What Verdocast actually is (grounding)

A **free-to-start, B2B-first football prediction game**. Anyone creates a league,
shares one link, a group (office / friends / creator community) predicts every
match *score*, and a **live, auto-scored leaderboard** ranks them. One backend,
three surfaces: **B2B office leagues** (primary revenue), a **£4.99 consumer
tournament pass**, and a **£5/mo editorial** lane. Wedge: **free group stage**
(acquisition) → **knockout-stage unlock** (conversion, from 28 Jun). Plus a
public **SEO/editorial** layer (WC2026 hub, group + team pages, knockout bracket,
match pages with line-ups/stats).

**Hard rule that defines us:** money buys *access to the game*, never a pooled
prize. Skill-based, free to compete, **never gambling**. Voice: *The Athletic,
not DraftKings*. (Source: `docs/PRD.md`, `CLAUDE.md`.)

## Step 3 - Side-by-side

| Dimension | **ADI Predictstreet** | **Verdocast** |
|---|---|---|
| **What it is** | Real-money prediction *market* (trading exchange) | Free social prediction *game* + editorial |
| **Core value** | Stake money on outcomes; win/lose cash | Shared fun + culture win; bragging rights |
| **Who it's for** | Individual, money-motivated fan | Groups: HR/offices, friends, creator communities |
| **Money flow** | You risk your own money on every trade | You never risk money; pay once for group access |
| **Content vs product** | Product only (a trading app) | Product **+** editorial/SEO surface |
| **Engagement model** | Personal, transactional, live-market | Group, social, leaderboard-as-watercooler |
| **Monetization** | Exchange economics on real-money volume | B2B per-league unlock; £4.99 pass; £5/mo sub |
| **Data & credibility** | Chainlink oracle; **official FIFA partner** | API-Football live scoring; independent editorial |
| **Brand voice** | Fintech/crypto "regulated markets" | *The Athletic* - analytical, inclusive, no gambling |
| **Distribution** | FIFA stadium boards; Kalshi/Fanatics rails | Viral share links, referral leagues, SEO, B2B GTM |
| **Regulatory load** | Heavy (Gibraltar licence, CFTC, geo-blocks) | Light by design (no pooled money = not gambling) |

### Where ADI genuinely wins
- **Brand legitimacy and reach at the top of the funnel.** "Official FIFA
  Prediction Market Partner" plus stadium boards on every broadcast is awareness
  we cannot match. They own the phrase "prediction market" in press.
- **Capital and partners.** Blockchain backer, Chainlink, Kalshi, Fanatics -
  serious distribution rails and money behind them.
- **A real revenue engine now.** Real-money volume monetizes from day one; our
  Stripe is still dormant pre-28-Jun.

### Where Verdocast wins
- **It's free to play and inclusive.** No stake, no loss, non-fans welcome. ADI
  is self-selecting for money-motivated bettors.
- **It's built for groups and workplaces.** ADI is fundamentally single-player
  and **cannot be run in an office** - a real-money trading app is a compliance
  and HR non-starter. That's our entire primary market, untouched by them.
- **No regulatory drag.** No pooled prize = not gambling. We ship anywhere; ADI
  geo-blocks the US and needs a three-entity workaround to touch it.
- **Editorial + product in one brand.** We earn trust and organic reach; they're
  a pure transactional app.

## Step 4 - So what: prioritized takeaways

**1. Don't chase them - contrast with them. Sharpen the "not gambling" wedge.**
ADI blurs "prediction" into real-money trading and calls it "informed
predictions, not betting." That muddies the word we own. Turn it into our
clearest line: **"Predictions, not stakes. Bragging rights, not losses."**
Put a plain "Free to play. No money in play. Not gambling." trust line on the
landing hero, join, and pricing pages.
*Why:* differentiation on the one axis buyers (HR especially) care about most.
*Metric:* landing → league-create conversion; B2B demo-request rate.

**2. Own the workplace lane they structurally cannot enter.**
A real-money app is banned from most offices by policy. Our B2B playbook is a
moat, not a coincidence. Push the office angle hard in July while WC attention
peaks. *Metric:* B2B leagues created; activation (≥60% predicting).

**3. Borrow their FIFA-halo tailwind, not their model.**
Their sponsorship is pumping "World Cup prediction" into public consciousness -
that rising search tide lifts us too. Make sure we capture it: WC2026 SEO hub,
knockout bracket, and match pages should rank for "world cup 2026 predictions /
predictor / office league." *Metric:* organic sessions to `/world-cup-2026` and
league-create from organic.

**4. Watch one real threat: brand/keyword confusion + a "free tier" pivot.**
Near-term the risk is a casual searcher landing on ADI thinking "predictions"
and never finding us. Longer-term, if ADI (or Kalshi) ever launched a free,
social, non-money prediction game to feed their funnel, that would overlap us.
Low probability - it cannibalizes their revenue model - but worth a quarterly
glance. *Action:* defend branded + generic "prediction league" SEO now; monitor
ADI/Kalshi product announcements.

**5. Steal one tactic: the live-market moment.**
Their strongest UX idea is *live, in-play* engagement tied to match moments. We
already poll the leaderboard live - lean into "watch it move" shares and
per-round live drama (streaks, "called the upset") as the free, social version
of that same thrill. *Metric:* live-match session length; public-standings share
rate.
