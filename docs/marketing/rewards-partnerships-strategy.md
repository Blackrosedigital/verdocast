# Points to Rewards: Partnerships Strategy

**Prepared by:** Ramon (Marketing) · **Date:** 6 Jul 2026 · **Status:** Decision brief

> **The one-sentence thesis.** Verdocast's leaderboard already crowns winners for free, on skill. Partnerships let a *brand* pay for what those winners receive - turning our reward layer into a revenue line and a distribution channel - **without ever putting player money into a pot.** The wedge we protect at all costs: free, no stakes, not gambling.

This doc is about the **sponsor-funded** lane and how to make it real.

A rewards mechanic that even *smells* like paying-to-win-cash would hand back our single clearest differentiator. So partnerships must make the reward feel like a **perk or a prize you earned**, never a payout you played for.

## 1. The core model: points to rewards without becoming gambling

**The shape that stays legal and on-brand:**

1. **Free to compete.** No entry fee is ever required to be eligible for a reward. (An admin paying to *unlock the knockouts* is fine - that is access to the game, not an entry fee for a prize pot. The reward itself must be reachable without any purchase - see the compliance note.)
2. **Winner decided by skill.** The leaderboard ranks predictions. Score-prediction is a game of skill; the outcome is not random. This is what makes it a *prize competition*, not a lottery.
3. **The reward is funded by someone other than the players** - a sponsor brand, the employer, or Verdocast. **Money never pools from players and never pays out to players.**
4. **The reward is recognition or a perk, not cash-for-play.** Vouchers, merch, experiences, status, donations. Never a cash prize framed as winnings, and never something you had to pay to have a shot at.

**Say it in one line (candidate brand copy):** *"Predict well, climb the board, win real rewards - free to play, no money in play."*

### The compliance lines to stay on the right side of (UK + general)

Brief, and **not legal advice - this needs a solicitor sign-off before any prize goes live** (PRD §17 flags this too):

- **Lottery (illegal without a licence) = payment + prize + chance.** Remove *chance* (skill decides) **or** remove *payment* (free entry) and it is no longer a lottery. We do both. Keep doing both.
- **The "no purchase necessary" / free-entry route must be genuine.** Under the UK Gambling Act 2005, if there is *any* paid path to a prize, there must be an equally prominent, non-disadvantaged free path. Cleanest position: **the reward is never gated behind the paid knockout unlock.** A player in a free league must be able to qualify for and win a sponsor prize. Do not couple "pay to unlock" and "eligible for the prize" - keep them structurally separate.
- **Prize competitions must require genuine skill** (Gambling Act, s.14). Our leaderboard does. Avoid tiebreakers resolved by pure chance; if you need a tiebreak, use a skill question or an earlier-round performance metric, not a random draw.
- **A "prize draw" is different from a "prize competition."** A *free* prize draw (random winner, no payment) is legal too, but random = weaker brand story and reintroduces the chance element. **Prefer the skill competition; use random draws only for free, low-stakes re-engagement** (the proposed sweepstake layer, PRD §6).
- **Consumer protection / advertising (CAP Code + ASA):** publish clear terms - who can enter, closing date, how the winner is decided, the prize, who the promoter is, and how the winner is notified. Sponsored content and prizes need disclosure (#ad / "in partnership with").
- **Employer-funded prizes** sit largely outside gambling law entirely (it is an internal staff incentive), which is why the B2B lane is the lowest-risk place to start. **Tax note for the employer, not us:** staff prizes can be a taxable benefit - we mention it in admin help copy, we do not advise on it.
- **Data:** any partner "eyeballs" value must respect that **member emails are platform-admin-only** (PRD §16). We can offer partners *audience and exposure*, never a list of employee emails. This is a feature - it keeps HR comfortable.

**The trap to name now:** the moment a prize's value scales up (say, a £2,000 experience), scrutiny scales with it, and the "was this really free and skill-based?" question gets asked harder. High-value prizes are fine - they just make airtight terms and the genuine free-entry path non-negotiable.

## 2. Reward types, ranked by fit

Ranked by leverage = (brand fit x ease to source x audience pull), and split by audience because the office and the consumer want different things.

| Rank | Reward type | Why it fits Verdocast | Best audience |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Brand discount codes / vouchers** | Near-zero fulfilment, infinitely scalable (every qualifier can get one, winner gets a bigger one), sponsor gets trackable redemptions. The natural *entry-level* partner ask. | **Both.** Perfect for consumer/global (mass distribution); good "everyone who qualified" perk for offices. |
| **2** | **Experiences (match tickets, hospitality, stadium/fan events)** | Highest desire, most on-brand ("The Athletic" reader wants access, not tat), unforgettable winner story = great content. Scarce, so reserve for the *top* prize. | **Consumer/global** headline prize; **B2B** as a flagship enterprise-league prize. |
| **3** | **Physical merch / kit** | Tangible, brandable (co-branded Verdocast x [brand] item), great unboxing/social content, works as both winner prize and milestone reward. | **Both.** Office: team-wins-a-hamper energy. Consumer: limited-run "Champion" kit. |
| **4** | **Digital perks / subscriptions** | Zero marginal cost to the partner, high perceived value, natural fit with our editorial lane (a media/streaming sub for football fans). Recurring exposure. | **Consumer** especially (individuals value a personal sub); office as a per-person perk. |
| **5** | **Status / recognition (badges, "Verdocast Champion 2026", shoutouts)** | Costs nothing, always safe, pure skill-flex, drives the viral "I'm top 10" share (PRD §7.4). The baseline reward under *every* prize. | **Both** - always on, everywhere. |
| **6** | **Charitable donation in the winner's name** | The most HR-safe, inclusivity-perfect, brand-halo reward. Removes any "prize greed" optics entirely. Great co-branded story with a partner. | **B2B** especially (culture win, zero tax/appropriateness worry); strong CSR co-brand hook for partners. |

**The pattern that ties it together - a tiered prize, one competition:**
- **Everyone who plays:** status/badges + a partner discount code (mass, cheap, scalable).
- **Top N qualifiers ("Q" spots):** merch or a bigger voucher.
- **The champion:** the experience / headline prize.

This is one skill competition with escalating rewards - easy to explain, easy to make compliant, and it gives *every* mid-table player a reason to climb (PRD §7.2).

## 3. Partner categories to pursue, and why they want in

**What Verdocast offers a partner (the pitch, four levers):**
1. **A highly engaged, opted-in audience** at the exact moment of peak football attention (the knockouts), returning daily to a leaderboard.
2. **Employer / HR distribution** - a channel into workplaces that is notoriously hard to reach cleanly, delivered by the internal-comms person who *wants* to share it.
3. **Co-branded leagues and prize exposure** - logo on the leaderboard, in the "you qualified" moment, in reward emails, in the winner announcement and its social shares.
4. **Brand-safe context** - non-gambling, inclusive, editorial-toned. A brand that will not touch a sportsbook *can* be here. **That is our unique selling point to sponsors.**

| Category | Example brands (illustrative) | Why they want in | What we give | What we get |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Sports retail / apparel** | JD Sports, Castore, Nike-adjacent, Football boots/kit retailers | WC audience = their core buyer; discount codes drive direct sales | Codes for all, kit for winners, co-branded merch | Scalable vouchers + physical top prizes + revenue share/flat fee |
| **Food delivery** | Deliveroo, Just Eat, Uber Eats | "Match-night order" occasion is perfect; codes drive first orders + reactivation | Per-qualifier credit, "match-day" milestone rewards | Cheap, high-volume rewards; a natural office perk (team lunch codes) |
| **Streaming / media** | A streaming service, a football media sub, our own editorial | Football fans = ideal subscribers; free-trial giveaways convert | Digital sub prizes; cross-promo in editorial lane | Recurring-value prizes at ~zero fulfilment cost |
| **Fan experience / ticketing** | Hospitality providers, fan-park operators, ticket resellers (legit) | Headline "money-can't-buy" prize = huge brand halo | The champion experience; winner-story content | Flagship prize, huge PR/share value |
| **Fintech (non-gambling)** | A neobank, a savings/rewards app, an expense tool | Want workplace distribution and a fun, positive brand moment - **but only if it's clearly not gambling** (that's us) | Co-branded office leagues; leaderboard sponsorship | Brand-safe sponsor $ + B2B co-distribution |
| **Coffee / drinks (non-alcohol-led)** | A coffee brand, an energy/soft-drinks brand, an alcohol brand *carefully* | Office ritual = coffee-run energy; "watercooler" fits literally | Milestone perks (a coffee on us), office hampers | Cheap recurring milestone rewards |
| **Football media / creators** | Podcasts, YouTube channels, The Athletic-style outlets | Want an engaged audience + a branded league for their community (PRD "creator" persona) | Co-branded creator leagues, `?ref=` attribution | Distribution + credibility + content |

**Note on drinks/alcohol:** an alcohol brand is possible but adds "appropriate at work?" friction for the B2B lane and inclusivity concerns. If pursued, **consumer/global only, never the office motion**, and never as the mechanic that drives play. Keep the office lane clean.

**Category to be wary of:** anything gambling-adjacent (sportsbooks, real-money "prediction markets" like ADI, fantasy-for-money, crypto-stake apps). **A single such partner detonates our entire "not gambling" position.** Non-negotiable no.

## 4. Mechanics: how partners plug in, and what's realistic when

**The plug-in surfaces (build-light to build-heavy):**

1. **Prize funding (no product change).** A sponsor simply funds the prize behind the *existing* global championship / per-league prize. The "league prize" admin field (PRD §7.5, item 2) already holds the winner-facing text. **This ships with almost nothing new.**
2. **Reward codes at milestones** (PRD §7.4 milestones). A discount code shown on "you qualified" / "perfect round" states and in a reward email (Resend infra exists). Light build: a codes table + a display surface.
3. **Co-branded leagues** (branding infra *already live* - PRD §9: per-league accent colour + logo). A partner runs a branded league for their audience via the existing creator/`?ref=` flow. **Zero new build - this is live today.**
4. **Leaderboard / championship sponsorship** ("The Verdocast Championship, in partnership with [brand]"). A logo slot on the global leaderboard + winner announcement. Small build.
5. **Referral rewards** - a partner code unlocked by inviting N players, feeding the viral loop. Post-launch.

**Realistic sequencing (we are mid-tournament; knockouts start 28 Jun - already live):**

| When | What's realistic | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Now (this tournament)** | **Co-branded creator/sponsor leagues** (live infra) + a **single manually-fulfilled sponsor prize** for the global championship (a spreadsheet + a human sending a code is fine at this scale). | Uses only shipped infra + the planned "league prize" field. No engineering blocker. |
| **Pre-final polish** | A basic **reward-code display** on qualifier states + a "Rewards & rules" page (PRD §7.5 item 5) with the compliant terms. | Small build, unlocks the voucher-partner category properly. |
| **Post-tournament / year-round (the expansion thesis)** | Productised **partner portal**: self-serve co-branded leagues, automated code distribution, leaderboard sponsorship slots, redemption analytics for partners. | This is the *real* business. The year-round PL/CL product (per strategy-expansion) is when a repeatable sponsor programme pays off. Don't over-build it for a 43-day tournament tail. |

**Honest constraint:** we are a 17-day-sprint MVP that is already live. For *this* World Cup, do **one** partnership manually and well. The scalable, automated version is a post-tournament build justified by the year-round roadmap, not this window.

## 5. The highest-leverage plays to pursue first (and the traps)

### Pursue first

**Play 1 - Land ONE headline sponsor for the Verdocast Championship (the global top-N finale).**
- **Why:** it turns the already-planned global championship (PRD §7.2) into a revenue line *and* a PR moment, with one negotiation instead of many. Proves the sponsor model with minimal build.
- **Build/ask:** a one-page sponsor deck (audience, engagement, brand-safe positioning, the "logo on the championship + winner story" package); the "league prize" field to hold the prize text; a manual fulfilment plan for one winner. **Ask the sponsor for:** the prize + a flat fee or the prize's cost covered. **KPI:** signed sponsor; championship-view engagement; share rate of the "I qualified" post.

**Play 2 - A voucher/discount partner for the "everyone who plays" reward.**
- **Why:** mass, cheap, scalable; gives *every* player a tangible perk (lifts activation and retention, not just the winner), and gives the partner trackable redemptions - the easiest "yes" in the deck.
- **Build/ask:** a batch of codes + a display surface on qualifier/milestone states + reward email. **Ask for:** a code pool + redemption tracking. **KPI:** code redemption rate; retention lift among code-holders.

**Play 3 - Co-branded creator/sponsor leagues, starting NOW (zero build).**
- **Why:** the branding + `?ref=` infra is already live. A football podcast or a brand can run a branded Verdocast league today. Distribution + credibility for free.
- **Build/ask:** a partner league checklist (logo, colour, join link, `?ref=` code). **KPI:** partner-attributed league creates and joins.

**Play 4 - Position employer-funded prizes as the safe default in the B2B motion.**
- **Why:** lowest compliance risk (internal staff incentive, largely outside gambling law), highest B2B engagement, no money-handling by us. It also *primes* the market for sponsor prizes - once admins see "our league plays for a prize," a sponsor-funded prize is an easy add.
- **Build/ask:** the "league prize" admin field (already planned) + help copy (including the "may be a taxable benefit" note). **KPI:** % of paid leagues that set a prize; their knockout return rate.

**Play 5 (post-tournament) - Productise the partner programme for the year-round platform.**
- **Why:** repeatable sponsor revenue only pays off across a season, not a 43-day tail. Line it up now, build it for the PL/CL relaunch.
- **KPI:** number of active partners; partner-driven revenue per season.

### Traps to avoid

- **Any cash-for-play or pooled-money mechanic.** Detonates the "not gambling" wedge - our single biggest asset vs ADI. Never.
- **Coupling the prize to the paid unlock.** If eligibility requires paying, the free-entry footing is at risk. **Keep "pay to unlock the game" and "eligible for the prize" structurally separate.** Free-league players must be able to win.
- **Random-draw prizes as the main mechanic.** Reintroduces "chance," weakens the skill story, and makes the brand feel like a raffle. Use skill; reserve free random draws for low-stakes re-engagement only.
- **Cheapening the brand with tat.** "The Athletic, not a cereal-box giveaway." A pile of low-quality branded junk erodes the premium, editorial tone. Fewer, better rewards.
- **Gambling/crypto-adjacent partners.** One sportsbook or real-money-prediction logo undoes the whole position. Hard no.
- **Over-engineering for this tournament.** Do one partnership manually and well now; build the portal for the year-round product.
- **Offering partners "data/eyeballs" that touches member emails.** We give exposure and audience, never PII. Selling access to employee emails would torch HR trust (our moat) and likely breach our own privacy commitments.
